Our household homelab

Family accounts.
Connected to home.

Docelar is our family's homelab. It brings personal accounts into privately run household services, with each family member choosing what to connect and share.

Our accounts belong to different people and different providers. Docelar brings them together for household use while keeping each person's identity, permissions, and choices distinct.

Each person's accounts, by choice

  1. Sign in as yourself. Your Docelar identity keeps your connected accounts separate from other family members' accounts.
  2. Connect with consent. Choose an account and review the permissions its provider asks you to approve.
  3. Choose household apps you trust. Using an app while signed in authorizes it to act for you: read connected calendars, manage your connections, and arrange unattended access. Disable or disconnect a connection to stop its use.

A homelab that can grow with us

Account connections start with Google. The aim is to bring more of the family's accounts and providers into Docelar over time, as our household needs evolve.

What a connection can do depends on the integration and the permissions you approve. New providers or expanded access will be explained before you are asked to authorize them.

A household app can retain received data and show it to other household members under its own practices. Background credentials you issue for an app act for you until expiry or revocation. Access for unattended household services requires separate consent and an expiry. Review these choices in the privacy policy.

Before connecting: read the privacy policy for data access, storage, sharing, and deletion, and the terms of use. For access or support, contact Docelar.
