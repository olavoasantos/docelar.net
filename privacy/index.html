Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Docelar is a privately operated household service for the family. It connects family members' external accounts for household use. This policy covers the public informational website and account connections; another household service may have additional practices that you should review before sharing data with it. Contact contact@docelar.net about privacy, access, or deletion.

Accounts and permissions

You choose which accounts to connect and approve permissions with each provider. Docelar keeps each person's account identity and access choices distinct. A household app acts on your connections as you when you use it while signed in, as explained below.

Google is the first available provider. The current Google integration uses the permissions described below. Read-only access is its current capability, not a permanent limit on Docelar's purpose. Other providers or permissions, including any future ability to change data, will be disclosed here and in the connection flow before you are asked to consent. Connecting today does not authorize those future capabilities.

Current Google connection: information accessed

  • Account identity: your Docelar account identifier, name and email when available, plus Google account identifiers and email to associate a connected account with its authorization.
  • Google authorization: the permissions you grant, authorization credentials needed to maintain your connection, and its expiry and status. Docelar never asks for your Google password.
  • Calendar data: calendars visible to your Google account and their identifiers, names, and time zones; events in requested calendars and time ranges, including identifiers, titles, status, dates, times, time zones, and recurrence information. Google may return other event details permitted by these permissions; the information currently passed to household services is limited to the fields listed here.
  • Access choices and activity: household apps acting for you, credentials and unattended access you authorize, selected calendars, permissions, expiry, sign-in information, and security records such as the requesting app, activity type, time, and outcome.

How connected-account information is used

We use account information and authorization to connect the correct person and account to Docelar, maintain the connection you requested, and provide household features you use or authorize. In the current Google integration, that means discovering calendars and answering authorized read-only Calendar requests. It does not create, edit, or delete events.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Docelar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Household apps and unattended access

Using a household app while signed in authorizes it to act for you, without a separate permission step for each app. It can read your connected calendars and events, manage your connections, and issue credentials and authorize access for unattended household services on your behalf. Managing connections includes connecting, reauthorizing, disabling, enabling, disconnecting, and removing them. Use only household apps you trust with this authority.

If you issue a background credential for an app, that credential lets it act for you within the access you selected for that app until it expires or you revoke it. Issuing the credential is your consent to that background use; signing out does not revoke it.

Access for a separate unattended household service requires separate consent, selected calendars and operations, and an expiry. A signed-in household app can arrange that access for you. The service can continue without an active browser session until its permission or credential expires or is revoked. Signing out does not revoke this access either.

Sharing and human access

Connected-account data is returned to you, household apps you use while signed in or for which you issue background credentials, and separately authorized unattended household services. Today, Google's connection returns calendar and event results. Your Google authorization credentials are not shared with household apps or services. An app may retain what it receives and show it to other household members under its own practices, even though those members cannot directly manage your connections. Review an app's practices and choose what to display or share before using features that expose your data to others.

Google data, including information derived from it, is used and shared only for the household features described to you. Household sharing must serve those features with your consent; it does not permit unrelated use of your Google data.

Docelar exchanges information with account providers to obtain your consent, maintain your connection, and use the features you authorize. Providers that deliver and protect this public website may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. They are not given your connected-account content or authorization credentials.

The operator administers the service and its protected storage. Human access to Google user data is limited to your specific agreement, necessary security or abuse investigation, or legal obligations. We do not provide Google user data to unrelated parties for their independent use.

Storage and retention

Docelar retains account details, your access choices, and limited security history to maintain and protect connected accounts. Google authorization credentials are encrypted in storage, with access restricted to administration of the service. The account-connection feature does not retain calendar and event results after returning them for a request. Household apps and services may keep copies of received data under their own retention and deletion practices, including after access ends. New integrations will have their data and retention practices disclosed before use.

Authorization credentials remain while a connection is maintained, until removed by disconnect or operator deletion. Account details, past permissions, and connection history can remain until operator deletion; revoking access does not erase all account records. Temporary sign-in records and security history are removed through routine cleanup, without a guaranteed deletion date.

Protected backups may retain earlier copies of account records, including encrypted authorization credentials, until those backups expire under the applicable retention schedule. Deleting current records does not immediately erase older backup copies.

Your choices and deletion

  • Stop using a household app and sign out to end its signed-in access. Because signed-in apps act as you without individual permissions, disable or disconnect a connection to stop access to that connection across household apps and services.
  • Revoke background credentials you issued for an app and revoke permissions or credentials for unattended household services to stop their future access. Signing out alone does not stop them. Revoking this access does not revoke your authorization with the account provider.
  • Disable a connection to pause its use, or disconnect it and revoke the provider's authorization. Revoking Docelar's access at Google can affect other Google permissions you previously granted to Docelar. If provider revocation cannot be completed, Docelar stops using the connection and reports the incomplete revocation.
  • You can also remove access using your Google Account connections settings.
  • For deletion of retained account, connection, or permission records, email the operator with the account identifier. We will verify the request before removing data. Do not send passwords or authorization credentials. Data already delivered to another household app or service must also be addressed with that app or service; stopping access does not erase its copies.

Cookies and website requests

Docelar uses necessary cookies for sign-in and account security. These public pages include no analytics, advertising, or data-entry forms. Website providers may use necessary security mechanisms.

Changes

We keep this policy aligned with the service. Before Google data is used in a new way or for a different purpose, we will notify you and ask you to consent to the updated policy.
