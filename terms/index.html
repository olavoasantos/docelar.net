Terms of use

Effective November 23, 2026

These terms apply when connecting accounts to the privately operated Docelar household service. By using Docelar's account connections, you agree to these terms and acknowledge the privacy policy. Questions: contact@docelar.net.

Invited use

Docelar is for family members authorized by the operator. Approving an external account connection does not itself grant access to the homelab. Connect only accounts and use only data you are entitled to access, and follow applicable laws and the terms of each provider and household service.

Your access choices

You choose which accounts to connect within the permissions offered by each integration. Using a household app while signed in authorizes it to act for you without a separate permission step for that app: it can read your connected calendars and events, manage your connections, and issue credentials and authorize access for unattended household services on your behalf. Current Google access is read-only; additional providers or capabilities, including any future write access, require their own disclosures and consent. Protect your login and credentials, and use only household apps you trust.

Background credentials you issue for an app act for you within the access selected for that app until they expire or you revoke them. Issuing a credential is your consent to that background use. Access for separate unattended household services still requires separate consent, selected calendars and operations, and an expiry; a signed-in app can arrange it for you. Signing out does not revoke background credentials or unattended access.

A household app or service may retain received data and show it within the household under its own practices. Review those practices and choose what to display or share before using features that expose your data to others. Google data and information derived from it remain subject to the privacy policy and its Limited Use commitments.

Stop using an app and sign out to end its signed-in access. Disable or disconnect a connection to stop its use across household apps and services. Revoke app background credentials and unattended services' permissions or credentials to stop their future access. You can also revoke Docelar's access in your Google Account connections settings; this may affect other Google permissions you previously granted to Docelar. Stopping access cannot retrieve data an app or service already received, erase its copies, or undo actions already completed.

Responsible use

Do not bypass access controls, impersonate another user or application, attempt to obtain another user’s credentials or data, overload the service, or use received Google data for advertising, sale, surveillance, or training generalized AI models. Respect the permissions and purpose for which access was granted.

Availability and changes

This is a privately operated homelab with no uptime or support-time commitment. Features can change or become unavailable, and account providers may require renewed consent or revoke authorization. Do not rely on Docelar as your only copy of important information or for safety-critical tasks. The operator may suspend access to maintain security, address misuse, or discontinue the service.

Ending use

You can stop using Docelar's account connections and revoke a provider's access at any time. Contact the operator to request deletion of retained records; backup and household-service limits are described in the privacy policy. Material changes to these terms will be communicated to family members before continued use is requested.
